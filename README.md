LUMEN is a survival arcade game: you are the last light in a dark world.
Your light drains as you move, so feed on light orbs to stay alive, and
shove the hunting shadows back with a burst — but a burst spends light too.
Chain orbs quickly to build a combo, survive the rising levels, and beat
your best score. Everything is drawn and sounded in code: no image or
audio files, no network, no library.

- Move: mouse or WASD / arrow keys — the wisp follows your pointer
- Burst: Space or click — a shockwave that flings shadows away
- Pause: P — Sound: M — Restart: R
- Light is health and fuel: orbs restore it, shadows drain it on touch
- Four shadow kinds (chaser, hunter, brute, splitter) arrive as the level rises
- Combo multiplier, level-ups, particles, screen shake, synthesized sound

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
